Get your game on GameGemGo

GameGemGo is a swipe feed of playable game previews — real gameplay the instant a card is on screen. Two ways to be part of it.

Add your game to the feed Free during early access

A normal spot in the feed — plain framing, no gold border, tracked for engagement and clicks exactly like everything else. While GameGemGo is in early access, getting featured this way costs nothing.

What we need:

  • One self-contained HTML file — CSS/JS/assets inlined, no external requests (it has to run inside a sandboxed <iframe sandbox="allow-scripts"> with no allow-same-origin, so no cookies/localStorage/parent-frame access)
  • 5MB hard cap, 2MB or under strongly preferred — every extra megabyte is real wait time on a weak connection before it's playable
  • A title, and a store or website link for what tapping the card should open
  • A contact email so we can reach you about it
