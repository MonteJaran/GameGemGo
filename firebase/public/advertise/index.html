Sponsor placements · demo pricing

Two ways to put your playable in front of players

GameGemGo sells two kinds of direct, prepaid sponsor placement — separate from ordinary ad-network inventory, which we don't set the price of. Both draw down a prepaid balance per click and per qualified engagement; neither ever charges more than what's left in the balance. Rates below are an indicative demo, not a signed offer — see the disclaimer at the bottom.

Promo vs Featured

The only things that differ are position, framing, and price — tracking, billing mechanics, and the sandboxed playable pipeline are identical.

  • Framing: plain — same as regular inventory
  • Best for: steady low-cost reach without a premium slot

This page is a demo, not an offer. Rates are illustrative planning numbers, not a signed rate card, and can change per deal (the underlying rateCardCents field already supports a different rate per sponsor). Nothing here is legal, tax, or financial advice — setting your own prices for direct-sold placements below what an ad network happens to pay is a standard, legal pricing decision (advertising is a service/intangible, so it falls outside price-discrimination statutes like the U.S. Robinson-Patman Act, which apply only to commodities); the two things actually worth a professional's review before real money moves are (1) VAT/invoicing once you're billing companies directly, especially cross-border in the EU, and (2) whatever exclusivity or minimum-rate clause a future ad-network partner's contract includes, since that contract — not general law — is what could restrict discounting your own direct-sold inventory later.
